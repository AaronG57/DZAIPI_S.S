About the school

A secondary school serving the Dzaipi community

Dzaipi Secondary School is located in Dzaipi, Adjumani District, in Uganda's West Nile region. Government education records identify the institution as a secondary school in the district.

The Ministry of Education and Sports has listed Dzaipi Secondary School as a mixed/day institution offering Ordinary Level education.

The school's EMIS/MIS identification number is 12034, while its post-primary placement code is 1011.

Recent reporting also shows that the school serves learners from both refugee and host communities in Adjumani District.

Verification note: Historical records consistently identify Dzaipi Secondary School as a government-aided public institution. Some current datasets use the term "partnership" for its USE status, while recent 2026 reporting describes it as a government grant-aided school. The profile therefore avoids making a stronger ownership claim than the sources support.

Facilities & infrastructure

Investment in the learning environment

Several independently documented projects have supported facilities at Dzaipi Secondary School.

LAB

Science laboratory block

Japan's Embassy in Uganda reports that a science laboratory block was commissioned at Dzaipi Secondary School in February 2019. The project included a solar PV system, school furniture and laboratory equipment.

$$

Japanese grant support

The Embassy of Japan reports a grant of US$101,605, equivalent to more than UGX 250 million at the time, for construction and equipment of the science laboratory project.

ICT

ICT facilities and club

Adjumani district ICT education records list Dzaipi Secondary School among schools receiving ICT support, and Uganda's ICT Clubs directory lists an ICT club at the school.

🏠

Student dormitories

Adjumani District reports that the earlier DRDIP programme supported construction of dormitories at Dzaipi Secondary School, alongside similar investment at Mungula Secondary School.

WASH

Water and sanitation improvements

A recent menstrual-health pilot in Adjumani selected Dzaipi Secondary School as one of its intervention schools. The project included improvements to handwashing facilities, water access and toilet facilities.

2026 development

Making school access easier for learners

Dzaipi Secondary School was among five Adjumani secondary schools that received bicycles in 2026 to address transportation barriers.

🚲

52 bicycles

Dzaipi Secondary School received 52 bicycles: 50 allocated for learners and two for school administration.

🛣️

Reducing travel barriers

The 2026 programme targets learners who may walk several kilometres between home and school, sometimes arriving late or missing lessons.

🔧

Bicycle maintenance

The programme also provided bicycle toolkits, safety training and bicycle management systems, with local mechanics trained for maintenance.

👨‍🏫

School leadership

Dzaipi Secondary School headteacher Jerome Amizaru publicly welcomed the intervention, identifying transport as an important barrier to learning.

🇪🇺

International partnership

The bicycle initiative involved Cycling Out of Poverty Uganda, ENABEL, Uganda's Ministry of Education and Sports, Belgium and the European Union.

🔄

Reuse by future learners

The programme provides for bicycles to be reassigned to other deserving learners after current beneficiaries complete their studies.

Community

Serving host and refugee communities

Dzaipi Secondary School operates in an area that hosts refugee communities and has been included in programmes designed to support both refugee and host-community learners.

3–6 km

Travel distances reported for some learners

Reporting on the 2026 bicycle programme found that many learners in the participating Adjumani schools were walking between three and six kilometres to school each day.

At Dzaipi Secondary School, school leadership and student beneficiaries said the bicycle intervention would help learners arrive earlier, attend lessons more consistently and reduce the effect of long journeys on their education.

Important: The 3–6 km figure refers to learners across the participating schools and should not be interpreted as the exact distance travelled by every Dzaipi student.

Research & verification

Sources behind this profile

Government sources were prioritized for school classification and identification. Institutional and reputable reporting sources were used for facilities and recent developments.

  • Uganda Ministry of Education & Sports Government placement records list Dzaipi Secondary School under selection code 1011, O-Level, mixed/day, Adjumani.
  • Uganda UNEB school records UNEB school-selection records identify Dzaipi Secondary School with P.O. Box 156, Adjumani, and selection code 1011.
  • Government school records Government-school datasets identify Dzaipi S.S. with EMIS/MIS number 12034.
  • Uganda Ministry of Education headcount Ministry records identify Dzaipi S.S. as a government-aided secondary school in Adjumani.
  • Embassy of Japan in Uganda Official Embassy reporting documents the construction and commissioning of a science laboratory block at Dzaipi Secondary School, including solar PV, furniture and laboratory equipment.
  • Adjumani District Local Government District reporting documents previous DRDIP investment in dormitory construction at Dzaipi Secondary School.
  • Nile Post — July 2026 Current reporting documents the 260-bicycle initiative in Adjumani and confirms that Dzaipi Secondary School received 52 bicycles.
  • UNICEF Uganda UNICEF reporting documents student participation at Dzaipi Secondary School in community-based programmes supporting girls' return to education.
